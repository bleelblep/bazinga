Batch three

Out November 5.

Speech Bubbles Android only

Chat as speech bubbles: your messages on the right, everyone else's on the left.

  • Bubble colours from your theme (or swapped), or your own; optionally a colour per person
  • Corner roundness from square to pill, tails on the bubbles, breaks between messages sent far apart, and optionally no profile pictures
  • Message times hidden, shown under the bubble when you tap it, or in a pop-up above it
  • Swipe left with two fingers to see the time of every message
  • Reactions as round pills in the bubble's colour
  • Spoilers covered in shimmering ink until you tap them
  • A send animation: your message flies up into the chat (off by default)
  • A typing bubble while someone is typing
  • On in DMs, and optionally in servers

Plugin Hub

A Plugin Hub section in Discord's settings, with shortcuts straight into the settings of the plugins you choose.

  • Pick which plugins go in the hub; star up to four favourites to show as big tiles at the top
  • Plugins you've uninstalled are spotted, and you can clear them out in one tap
  • Choosing plugins needs Valenced's Developer Mode switched on; using the hub doesn't

UI Tweaks Android only

Hide the parts of Discord you don't use, and choose what double-tap and swipes do.

  • Hide rows in the message long-press menu, items in the + menu and sections of Discord's settings, each picked from a list of what it has seen; an Edit menu row hides rows right in the menu
  • Hide the reaction bar, the GIFs or Stickers tab, Message Requests and Quests (display only: quests you started keep going)
  • Keep the picker open after sending a GIF
  • Double-tap and swipe on a message: reply, edit, react, copy, quote, mention, pin, forward, start a thread, open a profile, mark unread and more
  • Other people's messages can get a second swipe action too

Picture Links

Tap profile pictures and banners to open them full size, with save and share. Nothing to set up.

  • Profile pictures open in Discord's own viewer at full size, with save and share
  • Tap a banner to view it; long-press for an animated banner
  • Long-press a server profile picture to see the person's main one

A port of redstonekasi's Picture Links, as updated by Rico040.

Batch two

Out October 31.

Round Corners

Undoes the redesign's squares.

  • Servers, Home, Create and Discover round again; the selected server can stay round or use the classic selection shape
  • Send, emoji, media, gift and apps buttons as circles, and the message box as a pill
  • The bottom bar and the You tab's floating bar as pills
  • Folders, channel rows, search bars and segmented controls fully round
  • Cards, settings rows and voice tiles with their pre-redesign corners, on a switch of their own
  • Each group of shapes has its own switch

Sharp Corners Android only

The opposite: every corner in Discord sharp.

  • Buttons, the message box, the You bar, menus, cards, sheets, server icons and pictures
  • The chat too, since messages are drawn natively
  • "Circles too" also squares avatars, status dots and round buttons
  • Can't run together with Round Corners, and tells you if both are on

Text Animation Android only

Letters animate as you type and delete, with a smooth gliding cursor.

  • Typing: letters fade in, slide up, unblur, pop or tilt
  • Choose the speed, slide distance, and pop and tilt strength
  • Deleting: letters fall away, turn to dust, or just vanish
  • The cursor glides between positions, optionally with a stretchy trail
  • The message box only, or every text field (never password fields)
  • Try it out in settings
  • Needs Android 10 or newer

Character Counter

Counts your message's characters in a small square above the message box, and sends long ones as several messages.

  • Characters typed, characters left, or typed out of the limit
  • Always shown, or only near the limit; with or without the word "characters"
  • On the right, it moves aside for Discord's Jump to present button
  • Past the limit it turns red and says how far over you are
  • Past the limit, Send splits the message at a blank line, a line break or the end of a sentence, and sends the parts in order, each after Discord confirms the one before
  • A sheet shows the progress with a Stop button; a setting can show the parts first instead
  • Tap the square to see how it would split

My community pull request for this one was closed: another Character Counter was merged first. This is my version, split and all.

Pocket Browser Android only

A web browser inside Discord, in a new tab of the emoji picker.

  • Tabs, history, bookmarks and a speed dial; tabs can survive closing Discord
  • Search with DuckDuckGo (default), Google, Bing, Brave or Startpage
  • Open links from chat in it instead of leaving Discord
  • Pop videos out into a floating or picture-in-picture player that keeps playing when closed
  • Dark pages, desktop sites, text size, JavaScript and third-party cookies, each with a switch
  • Clear history or site data any time
  • Needs Valenced 1.2.4 or newer

Batch one

The seven plugins that opened the repo, on October 28. All work on Android and iOS.

Custom Timestamps

Choose how the time next to each message looks.

  • Calendar ("Today at 2:05 PM", or a date for older messages), relative ("5 minutes ago"), ISO 8601 (in UTC), or your own format
  • Custom formats use moment.js-style letters (dddd, MMMM Do YYYY, h:mm:ss a); put words in [brackets] to keep them as written
  • A second custom format for messages sent today, such as h:mm A for just the time
  • {day} says "Today" or "Yesterday" (the weekday on other days)
  • Settings list every format letter with an example, plus ready-made formats you can tap to use
  • A 24-hour switch for Calendar ("Today at 14:05")
  • Optionally shows the name, avatar and time on every message instead of grouping them

Based on Custom Timestamps by Fiery.

Hide Servers

Hide servers, folders and channels you don't want to see. Only your device changes: you stay in the server, and your server order elsewhere isn't touched.

  • Tap the eye at the bottom of the server bar, then tap servers to hide or show them; hidden ones are darkened until you tap the tick
  • The small eye on a folder's corner hides the whole folder
  • Hide a channel or category from its long-press menu
  • Bring anything back from a list in settings
  • Hidden servers' emoji still show in the emoji picker, unless you turn on "Hide their emoji too"

With blerp; one file adapted from kmmiio99o's ServerDrawer.

New Members

A sprout next to people who joined the server in the last week, like on desktop.

  • The badge as an emoji, a recoloured icon, or Discord's own
  • Any icon colour, or reset it to green
  • If someone's role has an icon, show the badge in its place, or keep the role icon and add 🌱 after the name

No Dividers

Removes the thin lines between rows in settings and other lists.

  • Nothing to set up; turn it off to bring the lines back

ReviewDB

Read and write reviews on people's profiles and on servers, from ReviewDB.

  • Reviews on user profiles and in server menus
  • Write, vote on, delete and report reviews
  • Block people whose reviews you don't want to see
  • Hide warnings or timestamps
  • Notifications about new reviews of you
  • Sign in with Discord's own Authorize screen: ReviewDB only sees your username and avatar, not your messages or servers

A port of Vencord's ReviewDB by mantikafasi and Vendicated (GPL-3.0).

Send Tweaks

Tidies your messages as you send them. Nothing in code blocks is touched.

  • Clean links: strips tracking from links (YouTube, Spotify, X, Instagram, Amazon and more), plus the ClearURLs rules, updated about once a week
  • Polish (off by default): capitalise sentences, add apostrophes, optionally end with a full stop
  • Your own rules: find-and-replace for text or links, for example X links to fixupx
  • Ready-made rules: one per site, each showing what it changes; new and fixed ones arrive without a plugin update
  • Rules can apply as you type; anything typing missed is caught when you send
  • Import and export your rules
  • {timestamp} becomes the current time, shown in each reader's own time zone
  • {rainbow:text} becomes rainbow-coloured text
  • Colour codes in ```ansi blocks are fixed up so the colours show
  • Silent messages (off by default): send every message without notifying anyone
  • Reply pings (opt-in): start replies with the @ mention off; you can still turn it back on
  • Hold and swipe send: let go halfway to preview the finished message, or swipe all the way up to send it exactly as typed; a plain tap can open the preview too
  • Edits: cleaning and rules apply to edited messages too, and you see the result as soon as you open one (can be turned off)

With Rosie and WarFade.

Timezones

See someone's local time next to their messages.

  • Set anyone's timezone by searching a place or from a list of zones
  • Their time, and optionally their day, beside their message times, in 12- or 24-hour
  • Reads the shared timezone list other clients use, so people who've added themselves show up on their own (can be turned off)
  • Can also read Equicord's timezone list (off by default, read only)
  • Set or change someone's zone from the ⋯ menu on their profile, or from the bottom of the message long-press menu (that one can be turned off)

A port of Aliucord's Timezones by aniimalz.

Pronouns

People's pronouns as a small tag next to their name in chat.

  • Read from their Discord profile (the server profile first), and optionally from PronounDB
  • "Only real pronouns" keeps just the pronoun sets from a profile field ("he/him 🌈 | 19" shows "he/him") and hides fields with none
  • Neopronouns included; any/all can be counted or not, and you can add words of your own
  • Choose who gets a badge: everyone, everyone except people you hide, or only people you pick
  • Set someone's pronouns yourself from the long-press menu on their message, or by user ID
  • One badge colour for everyone, each person's role colour, or a colour per person

Hide Call Buttons

Hides the call and video buttons you never use. Calls still work from everywhere else.

  • The call button on someone's profile
  • The call and video buttons at the top of a DM or group DM
  • The call button at the end of each row in the friends list
  • The camera button in the voice channel controls
  • Each button has its own switch

A port of John's (janisslsm) Hide call buttons for Vendetta.

Staff Tags

OWNER, ADMIN, STAFF, MOD, VC Mod, Chat Mod and WEBHOOK tags next to staff, worked out from their permissions in that server.

  • In chat, in the member list and on profiles
  • Hide or rename any tag
  • Give a tag a colour or a two-colour gradient
  • Add an icon (Discord's own or your own SVG), or show the icon alone
  • Optionally use the person's top role colour as the tag background
  • Reset any tag to how it started

A port of Staff Tags for Revenge by Fiery and シグマ siguma.

Live Markdown Android only

Formatting shows up in the message box as you type, instead of the raw **symbols**.

  • Bold, italics, underline, strikethrough, code, spoilers and more, styled as you type
  • Headings grow and -# subtext shrinks like in chat (can be turned off)
  • Fades the markdown symbols so the text reads clean
  • Code in a monospace font
  • Select text to get Bold, Italic and the rest next to Copy and Paste; tap one again to undo it
